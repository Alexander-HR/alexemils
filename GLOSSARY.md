# Wishlist

The Owner's ongoing list of gifts they'd like, which family members use to coordinate who gets what.

## People

**Owner**:
The person whose wishlist it is (Alexander). The only one who curates Wishes.
_Avoid_: Admin, user

**Gifter**:
A family member who browses the wishlist and claims Wishes.
_Avoid_: Visitor, user, buyer

**Guest**:
A Gifter who claims Wishes without logging in, identified only by the name they give.
_Avoid_: Anonymous user, visitor

## Wishes

**Wish**:
One gift the Owner would like, shown as a card on the wishlist.
_Avoid_: Item, gift card, product

**One-off Wish**:
A Wish that can be claimed by exactly one Gifter; once claimed it is taken.
_Avoid_: Normal wish, single wish

**Recurring Wish**:
A Wish the Owner is happy to receive many times (e.g. a bottle of wine); it is never taken and instead shows how many times it has been given.
_Avoid_: Repeatable, consumable, infinite wish

**Received**:
A Wish the Owner has been given; it leaves the wishlist but is kept in the archive along with its Claims.
_Avoid_: Done, completed, deleted

## Claiming

**Claim**:
A Gifter's declaration that they are getting a particular Wish.
_Avoid_: Booking, reservation (as a noun for the act)

**Reserved**:
The state of a Claim where the Gifter intends to buy the Wish but hasn't yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, planned

**Bought**:
The state of a Claim where the Gifter has the gift in hand.
_Avoid_: Purchased, done, fulfilled

**Spoiler Mode**:
The view in which Claims are visible; the Owner can turn it off to keep gifts a surprise.
_Avoid_: Admin view, reveal mode

**Spoiler Warning**:
The notice shown to anyone not logged in, before Claims are shown, letting them continue or go back.
_Avoid_: Popup, login prompt, gate

## Presentation

**Theme**:
The seasonal look of the site: Christmas, Birthday, or Standard in between.
_Avoid_: Skin, style, mode

**Personal Message**:
A short message from the Owner shown under the wishlist heading, for everyone who visits.
_Avoid_: Note (that's on a Wish), intro, bio
